# Ola Ride Booking Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Ola ride booking data using Power BI to uncover insights related to ride demand, booking status, customer satisfaction, revenue generation, cancellation patterns, and vehicle performance. The dashboard helps stakeholders make data-driven decisions by providing interactive visualizations and key performance indicators.

## Website 
https://ola-dashboard-app.streamlit.app/

## Objectives

* Analyze ride volume trends over time.
* Understand booking status distribution.
* Identify top-performing vehicle categories.
* Evaluate customer and driver ratings.
* Analyze ride cancellations and their reasons.
* Examine revenue patterns across payment methods.
* Identify high-value customers.
* Study ride distance and utilization patterns.

## Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Ride Volume Over Time

Analyzes booking trends across different dates and periods.

### 2. Booking Status Breakdown

Displays the distribution of successful, cancelled, and incomplete rides.

### 3. Top 5 Vehicle Types by Ride Distance

Identifies vehicle categories covering the highest total ride distance.

### 4. Average Customer Ratings by Vehicle Type

Compares customer satisfaction across vehicle categories.

### 5. Cancelled Ride Reasons

Analyzes major reasons behind ride cancellations.

### 6. Revenue by Payment Method

Shows revenue contribution from different payment options.

### 7. Top 5 Customers by Booking Value

Identifies customers contributing the highest booking revenue.

### 8. Ride Distance Distribution

Examines ride distance patterns and trip behavior.

### 9. Driver Ratings Distribution

Analyzes driver performance based on ratings received.

### 10. Incomplete Rides Analysis

Investigates incomplete rides and associated reasons.
